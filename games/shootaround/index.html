      How to play

      1. Tap (or press Space) to face the hoop. You keep facing it after that.
      2. Hold to jump.
      3. Let go at the top of the jump to shoot. Watch the bar: let go in the green.

      Time attack: score as many points as you can in 60 seconds. Shots from behind the arc are worth 3. Make 3 in a row and you're on fire!

      Sudden death: no clock. Keep shooting until you miss. One miss and it's over! Make 5 in a row and the green starts moving, so watch the bar.

      Around the world: score from all 7 spots around the key, in order, as fast as you can. Miss and you shoot from the same spot again. The green moves a little each shot.

      HORSE: 2 to 4 players on one device. The leader picks a spot and shoots. Make it, and everyone else has to make the same shot or get a letter. Miss, and the lead passes on. Spell HORSE and you're out. Last one left wins! The green moves for every new spot, and matching means the same spot and the same green.

      3-point contest: shoot every ball from 5 racks around the 3-point line in 60 seconds. Every shot is a three, and the last ball in each rack is a gold money ball worth double. The green moves every shot!

      Keep scoring for blue supernova fire at 10 in a row. Watch for a gold money ball (double points) and the rare rainbow ball (5 points)!

      Instant replay: after a game, tap Replay to watch your best shot again in slow motion.

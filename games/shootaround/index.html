    How to play

    1. Tap (or press Space) to face the hoop. You keep facing it after that.
    2. Hold to jump.
    3. Let go at the top of the jump to shoot. Watch the bar: let go in the green.

    Time attack: score as many points as you can in 60 seconds. Shots from behind the arc are worth 3. Make 3 in a row and you're on fire!

    Sudden death: no clock. Keep shooting until you miss. One miss and it's over!

    Around the world: score from all 7 spots around the key, in order, as fast as you can. Miss and you shoot from the same spot again.

    Keep scoring for blue supernova fire at 10 in a row. Watch for a gold money ball (double points) and the rare rainbow ball (5 points)!

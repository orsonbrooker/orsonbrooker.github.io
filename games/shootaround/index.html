How to play

  1. Tap (or press Space) to face the hoop. You keep facing it after that.
  2. Hold to jump.
  3. Let go at the top of the jump to shoot. Watch the bar: let go in the green.

Score as many points as you can in 60 seconds. Shots from behind the arc are worth 3. Make 3 in a row and you're on fire!
